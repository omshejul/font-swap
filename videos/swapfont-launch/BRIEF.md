# SwapFont demo

## Intent
A short product demo for the extension's marketing page and Chrome Web Store context. Show how picking two fonts changes the real webpage, then show simultaneous swaps. Keep the video focused on picking and instant replacement, without a dedicated undo or edit scene. The user requested Claude and the Motion Graphics Guide. Finish a rendered MP4 autonomously.

## Decisions
Landscape1920x1080 for website/YouTube context,24seconds and local silent-readable demo inferred from the active publishing task. English matches user's copy and remembered personal media default. No narration or paid generation. Use an upbeat original electronic soundtrack, synchronized clicks and transition lifts. Preserve its local generator.

## Assets
Five actual production extension screenshots in assets/, the actual rounded icon, locally bundled Inter fonts with OFL license, original local music and sound effects. No site scraping needed because all real product screenshots and researched facts are supplied. Product URL omshejul.com/app/swapfont. Store not published yet.

## Design
Charcoal#202124, pale#e8eaed, blue#8ab4f8. Plain sentence case, compact and legible. Typography and real picker UI lead. No em dash, allcaps subtitles, glass, gradients or fake UI.

## Delivery
renders/swapfont-demo.mp4 plus editable composition, keyframe contact sheet and ffprobe validation. No deployment or external publication by Claude.
