SwapFont lets you try different fonts on a live web page without changing its code. Click text, pick a Google Font, and watch it change. Free. Open source.

- Pick a font by clicking text on the page.
- Choose a Google Font and see it apply instantly.
- Add several swaps. Undo one or all.
- Remember a site, if you choose to.
- Use a floating panel or the side panel.
- Limit a swap to specific HTML tags.

Privacy
Page text, URLs, picker activity and saved swaps are processed locally, without behavioral analytics. They are not uploaded. Google Fonts receives the font you request. The catalog loads from omshejul.com. Google and Vercel receive connection details such as your IP address; Vercel may derive approximate location and retain operational logs. Feedback opens an editable email draft. Nothing is sent automatically.

Chrome 116+ on desktop. Normal HTML text only; browser pages, the Chrome Web Store, PDFs, images, canvas and inaccessible frames are unsupported.
