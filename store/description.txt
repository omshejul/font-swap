See what font a website uses, then try a different one.

Hover over text to see its font name. Click to pick it. Choose a new font and watch the website change right away.

What you can do
- Swap in a Google Font or another font already on the page
- Add several swaps, then undo one or all of them
- Remember your fonts for a site, if you like

Free. Open source.

Works on ordinary websites in desktop Chrome.

Your privacy
Page content, page addresses, font searches and saved settings stay on your device. SwapFont downloads the font list from our server and the fonts you request from Google Fonts. Downloads send your IP address and basic connection details.
